Interactive NDVI & Spectral Reflectance Lab

Class XI Formula

Healthy crops in Maharashtra absorb visible Red light (Sentinel-2 Band B04, ~665 nm) for photosynthesis and strongly reflect Near-Infrared light (Band B08, ~842 nm) due to healthy mesophyll tissue.

Near-Infrared Reflectance (NIR — Sentinel-2 Band 8) 0.70 (70%)
Visible Red Reflectance (RED — Sentinel-2 Band 4) 0.10 (10%)
Short-Wave Infrared Reflectance (SWIR — Band 11 for Humidity/NDMI) 0.25 (25%)

Step-by-Step Formula Evaluation

NDVI = (NIR - RED) / (NIR + RED)
NDVI = (0.70 - 0.10) / (0.70 + 0.10)
NDVI = 0.60 / 0.80 = 0.75 (Dense, Vigorous Crop)
NDMI (Canopy Moisture) = (NIR - SWIR) / (NIR + SWIR)
NDMI = (0.70 - 0.25) / (0.70 + 0.25) = +0.47 (High Canopy Humidity)

Class XI Exhibition Display Board (6 Mandatory Sections)

Structured directly according to your project document guidelines for Wagholi, Pune & Maharashtra agro-climatic zones.

SECTION 1

1. Introduction: Satellite Remote Sensing

Earth observation satellites such as the European Space Agency's Sentinel-2 capture multispectral imagery across 13 spectral bands at 10m–60m spatial resolution every 5 days. This enables non-destructive monitoring of crop vigour, surface humidity, and agricultural greenhouse gas fluxes across all 36 districts of Maharashtra.

SECTION 2

2. Scientific Principle: Red, NIR, NDVI & NDMI

Chlorophyll strongly absorbs visible Red light (665 nm) while healthy leaf cell structures reflect Near-Infrared (842 nm). Comparing these yields NDVI = (NIR − Red) / (NIR + Red). Combining NIR with Short-Wave Infrared (1610 nm) yields NDMI to track canopy water stress and surface relative humidity.

SECTION 3

3. Methodology: Maharashtra Region & Satellite Selection

Using the Copernicus Browser & Sentinel-2 L2A imagery, 152 agricultural sectors across Maharashtra (anchored by 32 sectors in Wagholi, Kesnand, Shirur, Baramati, Maval, and Junnar) were mapped across six cloud-free dates spanning DOY 102 (April) to DOY 287 (October).

SECTION 4

4. Observations: Multi-Date NDVI, Humidity & Emissions

Irrigated sugarcane and paddy plots along the Bhima, Krishna, and Godavari basins maintain high NDVI (0.74–0.88) and high canopy humidity (72–88% RH), accompanied by elevated methane (CH₄) fluxes. Dryland millet and cotton plots in Marathwada and eastern Pune show steeper moisture drops.

SECTION 5

5. Graph: Seasonal Vegetation Index Progression

Plotting NDVI against Day of Year (DOY) reveals a characteristic bell-shaped phenological curve: rising sharply during the Jointing Stage (DOY 169), peaking at the Tasseling Stage (DOY 205, NDVI ~0.82), and declining through the Maturity Stage (DOY 251) toward harvest.

SECTION 6

6. Conclusion & Field Verification Note

Satellite NDVI and moisture indices provide an early-warning system for irrigation scheduling and emission management across Maharashtra. Important Note: NDVI indicates greenness and vigour; combining satellite data with local weather records, soil type, and ground verification prevents misinterpretation.
